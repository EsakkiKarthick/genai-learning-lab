1 Employee Work From Home Policy
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Employees must remain available during normal working hours from 9:00 AM to
6:00 PM.
Work-from-home requests should be submitted at least one working day in
advance through the employee portal.
Employees working from home must have a reliable internet connection and
should use the company-approved VPN when accessing internal systems.


2 Remote Work Equipment Policy
Employees who regularly work from home may request company-provided equipment.
The company may provide a laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset based
on job requirements.
Equipment requests must be approved by the employee's manager and IT
department.
Employees are responsible for keeping company equipment secure while working
remotely.


3 Office Attendance Policy
Employees are expected to work from the assigned office location at least
3 days per week unless they have an approved remote-work arrangement.
Employees must follow their team's scheduled office attendance days.
Repeated failure to attend the office without approval may result in
managerial action.


4 Work From Home Internet Requirements
Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a stable internet
connection.
The minimum recommended internet speed is 50 Mbps.
Employees should use a secure Wi-Fi connection and avoid public Wi-Fi when
accessing company systems.
The company does not generally reimburse employees for their personal
internet connection.


5 Remote Work Security Policy
Employees working remotely must connect to company systems using the
company-approved VPN.
Multi-factor authentication must be enabled for all corporate accounts.
Employees must not share company credentials with family members or other
individuals.
Sensitive company information must not be stored on personal devices unless
explicitly approved by the security team.


6 Leave Policy
Employees should submit planned leave requests through the employee portal.
Leave requests should normally be submitted at least 3 working days before
the planned leave date.
Emergency leave may be requested without advance notice.
Managers are responsible for approving or rejecting leave requests.


7 Employee Internet Reimbursement Policy
The company does not normally reimburse employees for home internet
connections.
However, employees in certain approved roles may be eligible for a monthly
internet allowance.
Eligibility depends on job role, employment agreement, and manager approval.
Employees should contact HR to determine whether they qualify for the
internet allowance.
